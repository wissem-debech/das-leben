# Das Leben A1 + A2 – Review app

Interactive review app for the German textbooks *Das Leben* A1 and A2, organised by the books' units. Switch between **A1** and **A2** at the top of every page; progress is saved separately for each level.

**A1** (Start + Einheit 1–16)
- **Grammatik** – 49 rules with examples, common mistakes, quizzes, Plateau tests and a final test
- **Verben** – 147 verbs with Präsens, Perfekt, Imperativ (and Präteritum for sein, haben and modal verbs)
- **Wortschatz** – 435 nouns (A1 exam vocabulary) with der / die / das, plural and meaning

**A2** (Einheit 1–16)
- **Grammatik** – 45 rules in the order of the book's grammar overview (dass, weil, als, wenn, damit, um … zu, relative clauses, adjective endings, Präteritum, Passiv, Genitiv …)
- **Verben** – 173 new verbs with Präsens, Präteritum, Perfekt and Imperativ, plus their prepositions (sich freuen auf, warten auf …)
- **Wortschatz** – 345 new nouns from the A2 word list (A2 exam vocabulary; A1 words are not repeated)

Exercises: article trainer, word choice, flashcards, verb drills (Präsens, Präteritum, Partizip II, haben/sein, Imperativ, prepositions) and grammar quizzes. Interface in Arabic, English and French.
